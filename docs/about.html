About

A direct response writer with a mechanic's habits

I lead marketing communications at APMEX and its affiliated brands. I got here the long way, and it's why I work the way I do.

The story

For eight years I ran an auto repair shop in Stillwater, leading a crew of five to eight people. The job taught me to diagnose before I fix, to explain technical problems in plain words, and to earn the trust of customers who assumed they were about to be oversold.

When my kids were young I stayed home with them full time and started freelance copywriting: direct response, email, SEO and product copy, much of it for automotive brands like FIXD. That time at home taught me more than any job has. It's where I learned to manage my own pipeline, deadlines and revisions with no one checking in.

In 2023 I joined APMEX, one of the largest online precious metals retailers in the U.S. Today I lead marketing communications across APMEX and its affiliates: social strategy for three brands, press releases and earned media, campaigns, content, print and out-of-home advertising, email copy and analysis, and the AI-built tools my team uses every day. In my first months I was named Employee of the Quarter.

How I work

  • Diagnose first. Find what's actually broken before proposing a fix. The partner email review started with the tracking, not the creative.
  • Write for skeptics. Plain facts, no hype. If a claim can't be proven, it doesn't go in.
  • Plan channels together. Email, social, PR and content work best as one program.
  • Report the truth. A recap that names its weak spot earns more trust than one that only lists wins.

What I'm looking for

A full-time role leading email and lifecycle marketing, remote or in the Oklahoma City area. I'm especially interested in fitness, finance, supplements and e-commerce brands.
