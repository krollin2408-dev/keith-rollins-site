Dungeon Crawler Fitness

A fitness app that turns workouts into a dungeon crawl. It started as a board game I designed. Now I'm building its email program from zero, and documenting every step here.

Why this project is here

Most of my email work at APMEX was done with a team. This project is mine end to end: strategy, list growth, automation, segmentation, copy, testing and reporting. When it has results, they'll be real and they'll be here.

The game

Players complete quest cards made of bodyweight exercises to earn XP and gold, fight floor bosses, keep streaks alive and grow a five-skill character. The design is locked; a working prototype is being playtested.

The email plan

  1. Waitlist. An honest landing page on Kit. No fake scarcity, no invented testimonials, no results claims.
  2. Welcome sequence. Introduce the game, the creator and what's coming, and learn what each subscriber wants.
  3. Build-log newsletter. Regular updates from the build, so the list stays warm until launch.
  4. Segmentation. Tag subscribers by fitness level and interest to tailor launch messaging.
  5. Launch campaign. A paced sequence to turn the waitlist into first players.
  6. Reporting. List growth, opens, clicks and conversions, published here.
