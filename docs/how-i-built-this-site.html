How I built this site

Built with AI. Checked by me.

This site is part of my work. I made it with Claude as a working partner, and I made every call about what's true and what goes in.

What AI did

  • Pulled my author pages and counted my published work: 63 APMEX articles and 20 FIXD guides.
  • Read public view counts to find which of my video ideas outperformed.
  • Drafted pages from my notes and wrote the code: plain HTML and one stylesheet, hosted free on GitHub Pages.

What I did

  • Set the rule that every project names my exact role, and that every number has a source.
  • Cut what wasn't mine, corrected what was off, and chose the design.
  • Approved every word before it went live.

Why it matters

Using AI well isn't about typing prompts. It's knowing what to hand off, describing it clearly, checking the result, and owning what ships. Anthropic calls that AI Fluency, and this site is a working example of it.
