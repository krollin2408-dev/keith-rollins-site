What AI did, and what I did

  • Research. Claude pulled my author pages and counted my published work: 63 APMEX articles and 20 FIXD guides. It read public view counts to find which of my social formats outperformed.
  • Honesty rules. I set the rule that every project names my exact role and every number traces to a source. Where I supported a team, the page says so.
  • Writing. Claude drafted from my notes and case study kit. I corrected roles, cut what wasn't mine and approved every claim.
  • Code. Plain HTML and one stylesheet, no framework, so it loads fast and runs anywhere.

Why it matters

AI Fluency isn't typing prompts. It's knowing what to delegate, describing it clearly, checking the result and owning what ships. This site is a working example.

Built for

  • A 60-second skim: the home page leads with who I am and the numbers.
  • Depth on demand: 20 case studies, one template.
  • Phones, dark mode and screen readers.
  • Search: page titles, descriptions and structured data.
