Work/Social

Always-On Social and Content

Campaigns get attention, but most of a brand's presence is the steady work between them. That work needs structure so it doesn't depend on anyone's memory.

My role
Social media and content lead
Brand
APMEX and affiliates
Year
2023–2026
Status
Ongoing

The programs

  • A fixed weekly cadence. Monday market update, Tuesday and Thursday educational articles, Wednesday and Saturday reels, Friday deals, no Sunday post. Every campaign sits on top of this rhythm.
  • A map of every account. A handle roster for every brand in the group and its partners, organized by management tier.
  • Community activation. Leading the reactivation of APMEX's dormant Reddit profiles, where the brand is often discussed.

YouTube support

On APMEX's 186K-subscriber channel I manage the publishing schedule and write the SEO description for every video, more than 190 over three years. I've also run thumbnail and title A/B tests.

Results

  • A dependable rhythm across brands.
  • Clear ownership of every account.
  • SEO descriptions on 190+ videos.

Tools: Sprout Social, Jira, YouTube Studio
