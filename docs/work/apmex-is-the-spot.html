APMEX Is the Spot

APMEX sold silver dollars at spot price for one week. I built the campaign so the explanation would outlive the offer.

My role
Campaign lead
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026
Status
Completed

The challenge

"At spot" is a strong offer, but many newer buyers don't know what it means. A one-week promotion also leaves little behind once it ends. Leadership wanted to know whether the campaign moved anything beyond sales.

What I built

  • Evergreen explainer. What Does "At Spot" Mean? answers the question plainly and keeps working in search and AI answers.
  • Short-form video. A 76-word, four-scene vertical script, written to run again with any future at-spot offer.
  • Press release. This Week Only: APMEX Offers Silver Dollars at Spot, distributed through Notified.
  • Measurable social. Campaign posts tagged in Sprout Social so the campaign could be measured on its own.
  • Leadership recap. A deck and companion email covering AI search discoverability, earned media and week-over-week social change.

The honest part

The campaign made the week bigger, not better per post: at-spot posts had a lower engagement rate than our other content. I said so in the recap and recommended fewer, sharper at-spot posts that borrow the format of our best-performing market update series.

Results

  • Campaign posts drove nearly two-thirds of the account's link clicks for the week.
  • Total link clicks rose about 25% week over week.
  • Net follower growth rose about 33%; impressions and engagements grew by double digits.
  • Campaign posts drew roughly half of all tagged-post impressions.

Tools: Sprout Social, Notified, PowerPoint
