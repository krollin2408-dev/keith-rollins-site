APMEX Is the Spot

A one-week at-spot promotion, built so the explanation would outlive the offer.

My role: Campaign lead
~2/3of the week's link clicks came from campaign posts

The brief

APMEX sold silver dollars at spot price for one week. "At spot" is a strong offer, but plenty of newer buyers don't know what it means, and a one-week promotion leaves little behind once it ends.

What I did

  • Wrote an evergreen explainer, What Does "At Spot" Mean?, that keeps working in search and AI answers.
  • Wrote a 76-word vertical video script that can run again with any future at-spot offer.
  • Wrote and distributed the press release through Notified.
  • Tagged every campaign post so the campaign could be measured on its own, then wrote the recap for leadership.

What happened

  • Campaign posts drove nearly two-thirds of the account's link clicks that week.
  • Total link clicks rose about 25% week over week, and net follower growth rose about 33%.
  • One thing to improve: engagement per post was lower than our other content. I said so in the recap and recommended fewer, sharper at-spot posts next time.
