Work/Strategy & brand

Two Brands, Two Voices

APMEX has to sound like a steady authority. OneGold has to feel new and inventive. With many writers and formats, both voices drift unless someone holds the line.

My role
Brand voice steward
Brand
APMEX and OneGold
Year
2023–2026
Status
Ongoing

APMEX: the Ruler

  • No exclamation points and no superlatives.
  • No predictions about market direction.
  • No defensive framing; state the facts.
  • A defined list of banned words and phrases.

OneGold: the Magician

Inventive and forward-looking, supported by a Ruler, Rebel and Sage persona framework. The two frameworks stay fully separate.

In practice

Weekly market update captions compare spot prices with the prior Monday's close and never forecast. Trustpilot review responses stay calm and specific. Holiday video concepts were ranked partly on voice fit.

Results

  • Two recognizable brands that stay distinct.
  • Standards concrete enough for any writer to apply.

Tools: Brand archetypes, editorial standards
