Bullion Card Activation Series

New Bullion Card holders earn bonus points by hitting a spend target before a deadline. The series gets them to the first purchase, keeps them moving and closes before time runs out.

My role

I wrote the messaging for all eight emails. The email specialist designed and built them. We partnered on sequencing and urgency.

How the series works

  • Urgency on a schedule. The deadline tightens from 60 days to 45, 25 and finally 5.
  • A goal you can see. A personalized progress bar shows the dollars left before the bonus.
  • A reason to buy now. A 72-hour countdown offer with featured products drives the first spend.
  • Depth, not just pressure. The midpoint email cross-sells AutoInvest and the Knowledge Center.
  • Variants. Different product pairings, with and without the progress tracker.

What I'd test next

  • Subject lines that lead with points remaining versus days remaining.
  • A re-engagement branch for cardholders who stall halfway to the target.
  • Send-time testing for the 72-hour offer.

Results

  • A complete lifecycle journey: offer, urgency, progress and reward.
  • Dynamic content (progress bar, countdown timer) in every stage that needed it.
  • Cross-sell into recurring purchases built into the middle of the series.

Tools: Copy in partnership with the email specialist
