Eight emails to a first purchase

A five-stage lifecycle series that moves new cardholders from approval to their first bonus.

My role: Copy and messaging; designed and built by the email specialist

Scroll inside each email to read the whole send.

The brief

New Bullion Card holders earn bonus points by hitting a spend target before a deadline. The series had to get them to a first purchase, keep them moving, and close before time ran out.

What I did

I wrote the messaging for all eight emails across five stages, and partnered with our email specialist on the sequencing. The deadline tightens from 60 days to 45, 25, and 5. A personalized progress bar shows the dollars left to the bonus, a 72-hour countdown offer drives the first purchase, and the midpoint email cross-sells AutoInvest and the Knowledge Center.

What I'd test next

  • Subject lines that lead with points left versus days left.
  • A re-engagement branch for cardholders who stall halfway.
  • Send-time tests for the 72-hour offer.
