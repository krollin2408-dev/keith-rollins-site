Coinage Magazine Print Ad

Coin hobby print readers are knowledgeable and hard to impress. A generic "shop with us" ad would be ignored.

My role
Concept and copy lead
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026
Status
Delivered

Research first

I researched print placements for two audiences, precious metals investors and pop-culture coin buyers, to learn what each responds to and which titles reach them.

Proof, not claims

The ad centers on two facts collectors care about: APMEX is a U.S. Mint authorized purchaser, and its graded inventory is certified by PCGS, NGC and CAC. Composed and specific, with no superlatives.

Results

  • A full-page ad built on trust signals the audience respects.
  • Placement research covering two collector audiences.

Tools: Audience research, print copywriting
