A full-page ad for skeptics

A full-page Coinage magazine ad for collectors who have seen every pitch.

My role: Concept and copy

The brief

Coin collectors who read Coinage know more than most salespeople. A generic "shop with us" ad would get flipped past. The ad had to earn attention with facts this audience already respects.

What I did

I researched print placements for two audiences, precious metals investors and pop-culture coin buyers, then wrote an ad built on proof instead of adjectives. It leads with two facts serious collectors care about: APMEX is an authorized purchaser of the U.S. Mint, so new American Eagles are available the day they're released, and its graded coins are certified by PCGS, NGC and CAC.

Why it works

  • The headline names the reader: the serious collector.
  • Every claim is checkable, with no superlatives.
  • One clear next step, with a QR code for readers holding the magazine.
