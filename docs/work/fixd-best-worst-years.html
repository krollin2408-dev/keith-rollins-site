FIXD Best & Worst Years Guides

As a freelancer I wrote FIXD's Best & Worst Years guides: long-form, data-heavy articles that help shoppers pick a used car and introduce them to the FIXD sensor.

What's in each guide

  • A summary table of the best and worst model years.
  • Charts on reliability, safety ratings, fuel economy and value versus repair cost.
  • Year-by-year reviews covering common trouble codes and recalls.
  • Q&A on engines, transmissions and high mileage.
  • Calls to action for the FIXD sensor and related guides.

My decade in auto repair is why these read like advice from a mechanic.
