Holiday Social and Video Season

The holiday stretch stacks Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Giving Tuesday and the 12 Days of Deals close together. The plan had to bring holiday energy without breaking a composed brand voice.

My role
Season owner
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026
Status
In production

The lineup

Halloween, a run of daily at-spot drops before Black Friday, Black Friday through Cyber Monday, Giving Tuesday and the 12 Days of Deals. A People's Choice poll lets followers pick one of the 12 deals.

The video plan

  • 27 videos from mid-October to December 23, placed on the standing Wednesday and Saturday reel slots.
  • Four phases: Warm-up, Holiday Shop, Cyber Week and Countdown.
  • Concepts ranked by brand-voice fit. Unboxing videos lead because they are product-first and need no hype.
  • One shoot day at the Mint covers three November videos and the sound for a fourth.

Results

  • Every slot assigned and every concept tied to the brand voice.
  • Production tickets ready for the video team.
  • Open questions (KPIs, prize approvals) listed up front.

Tools: Jira, Sprout Social
