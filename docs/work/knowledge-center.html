What I write

The Knowledge Center is where APMEX teaches buyers and collectors. My articles turn monetary history and economics into plain English, then point readers toward their next step. They include a 12-part How to Sell series that supports the buyback program, explainers on topics like quantitative easing and the CPI, and dozens of history pieces.

A favorite

You Bought Your First Gold Bar, Now What? is written for first-time buyers coming from big-box stores. It works like a welcome email series in article form: storing it, tracking its value, buying more, and selling, with a clear next step at each stage.
