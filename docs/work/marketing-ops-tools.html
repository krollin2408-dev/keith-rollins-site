Marketing Operations Tools

A marketing communications role runs on information spread across a dozen places. I built tools that bring it to me each morning.

My role
Designer and builder
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026

What I built

  • Home Base. A start page refreshed every weekday morning with listening highlights, social and YouTube numbers, a to-do list and priority emails sorted by urgency.
  • Communications Calendar. A page that reads live from the team spreadsheet and leads with this week and next.
  • Social listening digest. A daily email of Brand24 highlights, with deeper dives into community threads about our brands, pricing and service (in setup).
  • Live social dashboard through the Sprout Social API (in progress).

AI Fluency

I built these with Claude as a working partner, and I've completed Anthropic Academy coursework including Claude 101. The skill isn't typing prompts; it's knowing what to delegate, describing it clearly and checking the result.

Results

  • Less time gathering, more time doing.
  • Nothing from the inbox gets lost.
  • The team calendar visible without another meeting.

Tools: Claude, Brand24, Sprout Social API, spreadsheets
