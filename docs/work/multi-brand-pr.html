Multi-Brand PR Expansion

APMEX built an earned media program on Notified and Qwoted. The group's other North American brands were barely using either.

My role
Plan owner
Brand
Bullion International Group
Year
2026
Status
In progress

The plan

  • Allocation, not equal shares. An annual release allocation weighted toward the flagship, drawn from a shared pool of credits.
  • Fit before platform. APMEX and OneGold suit expert-source pitching; other brands may only need the wire.
  • Start with one brand. A pitch presentation showing what APMEX achieved on both platforms, then regroup.
  • Renew early. Renew the wire contract ahead of its lapse with volume planned in advance.

Results

  • A brand-by-brand path to scale earned media.
  • A model I plan to reuse for a multi-brand YouTube strategy.
