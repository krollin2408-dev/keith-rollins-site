OneGold Card Launch

OneGold, an APMEX affiliate, launched a card in a category most people have never heard of. The content had to explain it fast and sound unlike APMEX.

My role
Copywriter and social strategist
Brand
OneGold
Year
2026
Status
Delivered

Brand first

OneGold uses a Magician archetype with a Ruler, Rebel and Sage persona framework. I kept it fully separate from APMEX's composed voice so the two brands never blur.

What I delivered

  • Video in two packages. Spoken-dialogue scripts and a silent-cut set built on visual storytelling and sound design, so the ads work with or without sound.
  • A 12-post launch arc that builds as a story instead of repeating one announcement.
  • Native advertising for Kiplinger, written to read as useful editorial for a personal finance audience.
  • OneGold 2.0 rebrand social launch strategy deck.
  • Clean tracking. A UTM convention for every launch link.

Results

  • A complete launch content system in one consistent voice.
  • Scripts that work with and without sound.
  • Every launch post traceable in analytics.

Tools: Brand archetypes, scriptwriting, UTM tracking
