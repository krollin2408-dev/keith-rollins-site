Which emails actually paid for themselves

An audit of paid partner email that showed what was working, and what the tracking was hiding.

My role: Analyst and report author
7 sendsaudited; 3 hidden tracking gaps found

The brief

APMEX pays a coin pricing publication to email its collector audience. Every send costs money, but nobody could say which ones paid for themselves, because the numbers lived in two places that didn't agree.

What I did

I pulled all seven sends into one view: delivery, opens, clicks, click-to-open rate, list health and revenue. Then I compared the creative, and checked the email clicks against our own site data to see whether the tracking could be trusted.

What I found

  • The send with the offer in the subject line had the best open rate.
  • Fewer than half the sends covered their cost in tracked revenue.
  • Three tracking problems were hiding results: shared campaign tags, links losing their tracking codes, and purchases going unrecorded.
  • I set a unique tracking tag for every send and a break-even bar each one has to clear.
