Podcast Guest Outreach Sequence

The Market Pulse Podcast needs experts who can argue both the bull and bear case for metals. Booking was feast or famine. I built a pipeline.

My role
Guest outreach lead
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026
Status
In progress

The shortlist

I reviewed past guests against the show's guidelines and filled the gaps: monetary economists and historians, academic researchers on gold, and mint and sovereign program leaders. Every name has a fit rationale, a bull or bear angle, and watch-outs such as conflicts of interest.

The sequence

  • Two waves. Tier 1 gets first contact in week one. Tier 2 opens in week three, so first-wave replies set the pace.
  • Three templates plus a variant for mint and sovereign program leaders.
  • Credibility in the ask. Each email states the topic, names the host, offers the questions in advance and includes audience proof.
  • Target: two to three confirmed recordings.

Writing sample: first-touch email

Subject: Invitation: Market Pulse Podcast conversation on [specific topic]

Dr. [Last name],

I'm Keith Rollins, Marketing Communications Lead at APMEX. Our Market Pulse Podcast is hosted by our Head of Media Relations and focuses on balanced, fact-driven conversations about precious metals, with no hype and no sales pitches.

I'm writing because of your [work / book / recent comments]. We would value your view on [one specific question], including where you see risks as well as opportunities.

The recording is remote, about 30 to 60 minutes, and we can share the question outline ahead of time. Recent episodes include [episode title and link].

Would you be open to a conversation? I can send a few times or our booking link.

Results

  • A repeatable guest pipeline with clear criteria.
  • A paced sequence that protects the show's "experts, not promoters" stance.
  • Templates any teammate can run.

Tools: Research, email copywriting
