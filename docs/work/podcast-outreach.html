A four-week email sequence to book experts

A paced outreach sequence to book credible guests for the Market Pulse Podcast.

My role: Outreach lead
11vetted experts, two waves, three templates

The brief

The Market Pulse Podcast wants experts who can argue both sides of the metals market, not promoters. Booking guests was feast or famine.

What I did

I built a shortlist of 11 experts, each with a reason they fit and anything to watch out for. Then I wrote a four-week, two-wave email sequence: the first wave goes out in week one, the second in week three, so early replies set the pace. Every email names the topic and the host and offers the questions in advance.

The first email

Subject: Invitation: Market Pulse Podcast conversation on [specific topic]

Dr. [Last name],

I'm Keith Rollins, Marketing Communications Lead at APMEX. Our Market Pulse Podcast is hosted by our Head of Media Relations and focuses on balanced, fact-driven conversations about precious metals, with no hype and no sales pitches.

I'm writing because of your [work / book / recent comments]. We would value your view on [one specific question], including where you see risks as well as opportunities.

The recording is remote, about 30 to 60 minutes, and we can share the question outline ahead of time. Recent episodes include [episode title and link].

Would you be open to a conversation? I can send a few times or our booking link.
