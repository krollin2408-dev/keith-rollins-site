Sprout Social Rollout

Social ran across many brands, networks and logins. I picked the platform to unify it, set it up and made campaigns measurable inside it.

My role
Selected and implemented
Brand
APMEX and affiliates
Year
2023–2026
Status
Ongoing

What I did

  • Selected Sprout Social and implemented it company-wide.
  • Connected every network possible: Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads and Reddit, with more coming.
  • Campaign tagging so a campaign like APMEX Is the Spot can be measured apart from the rest of the account.
  • Listening paired with Brand24 to monitor brand sentiment and catch engagement opportunities.
  • Live dashboard of social and YouTube numbers through the Sprout API (in progress).

Results

  • One platform for publishing, listening and reporting.
  • Campaign-level measurement that leadership recaps now rely on.
  • A foundation for the group's other brands to join.

Tools: Sprout Social, Brand24
