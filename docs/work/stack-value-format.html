The Stack Value Format

Precious metals content tends to be charts and commentary. I developed a format anyone understands in one second: a real stack of metal, and its dollar value.

My role
Strategy and writing
Brand
APMEX
Year
2025–2026
Status
Ongoing

My role

I developed the concept and wrote the videos. The APMEX video team produced them.

Why it works

  • Instant comprehension. A stack and a number need no explanation.
  • Built-in tension. "What it was worth then, what it's worth now" creates a reason to watch to the end.
  • Repeatable. Any product, any price move, any platform.
