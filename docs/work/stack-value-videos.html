What a stack of silver is worth

A short-form idea anyone gets in one second: a real stack of metal, and its dollar value.

My role: Concept and scripts; produced by the APMEX video team
420Kviews on the top video, 440 times the channel's typical video

The idea

Precious metals content tends to be charts and commentary. I came up with a simpler format: show a physical stack of metal and put a price on it, or show what the same stack was worth then and now. I wrote the videos; our video team shot and edited them.
