Tampa Digital Billboard Plan

APMEX had a fixed three-month budget to test digital out-of-home. Leadership needed to know where to spend it and how results would be measured.

My role
Strategist and author
Brand
APMEX
Year
2026
Status
Proposal

Groundwork

An executive research deck on digital out-of-home: market selection, buying models, vendors, tracking and KPIs, format and location.

The recommendation

  • Three digital bulletins in Tampa for a 12-week flight, early November through late January.
  • An interstate board as the anchor: more than half of total impressions at the lowest CPM.
  • A second board with the strongest audience match by a wide margin.
  • A third board extending reach, flagged honestly as about twice the cost per impression.

Built for a fast decision

If budget tightened, the first two boards alone keep roughly three-quarters of the impressions for about 60% of the cost. All three include attribution so results go beyond impressions.

Results

  • A plan leadership can approve or trim in one meeting.
  • A ready-made lower-cost option.
  • Measurement built in before booking.

Tools: Media planning, CPM analysis, vendor evaluation
